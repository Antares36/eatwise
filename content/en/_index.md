How much food do we throw away without even realising it? Food waste is not only about what ends up in the bin. It also represents the natural resources, energy, labour and money that were needed to produce that food.

In Greece, approximately 2 million tonnes of food are wasted every year, that is 201 kg per person. Nearly half of this comes from households, amounting to around 89 kg per person annually. And the most striking part? About 44% of this waste could be prevented.

Change begins where we make everyday decisions: how we shop, how we organise our fridge, how we store food, how we cook, and how we make use of leftovers.

EATWISE brings scientific knowledge closer to everyday life, through practical tools, educational resources, and reliable information for healthier, more sustainable, and more responsible food habits.

To learn what we waste. To understand why it happens. And to change, together, the everyday habits that make a real difference.
