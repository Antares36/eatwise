The EATWISE Course Module is being developed for university students and a wider audience of professionals and stakeholders interested in food waste prevention and healthy, sustainable food consumption. It will include digital educational content, infographics, videos, open-access scientific articles and activities that encourage active engagement and the practical application of knowledge.
