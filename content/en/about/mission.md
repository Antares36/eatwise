## Mission

EATWISE aims to provide citizens, schools, professionals and organisations with scientifically validated knowledge and practical tools needed to reduce food waste and adopt healthier and more sustainable dietary habits. Through awareness-raising and educational activities, it seeks to translate scientific knowledge into simple, applicable solutions for everyday life.

## Vision

EATWISE envisions a society in which food is used responsibly, and everyday dietary choices contribute to health, environmental protection and social wellbeing. In this way, the transition towards fairer, healthier and more sustainable food systems is supported. Change does not require perfection. It begins with small, conscious choices that can be repeated every day and, gradually, lead to meaningful and measurable outcomes.
