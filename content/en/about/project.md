## Bringing science to everyday food choices

The EATWISE project, with its full title “Education Against Waste: Promoting Behavioural Change for Healthy and Sustainable Food Consumption and Food Waste Reduction,” is implemented by Harokopio University within the framework of the 6th Call of the H.F.R.I. “Science and Society” Action entitled “Contemporary Nutritional Awareness” (H.F.R.I. Project Number: 29364). It is a 24-month research project that seeks to bridge scientific research with education and public awareness, contributing to the prevention of food waste and the adoption of healthy and sustainable dietary habits.

Food systems are responsible for 21–37% of global anthropogenic greenhouse gas emissions, while agriculture accounts for approximately 70% of global freshwater withdrawals. When food is discarded, it is not only the food itself that is lost; the water, land, energy, labour and financial resources required for its production and delivery to our plates are also wasted. In the European Union, 64% of food waste occurs at consumer level, with households responsible for 53% of the total. In Greece, food waste is estimated at approximately 2 million tonnes annually, or nearly 201 kg per capita. At the same time, millions of people lack access to sufficient and nutritious food, while undernutrition, micronutrient deficiencies and obesity coexist across populations, forming the “triple burden of malnutrition.” Food waste is therefore not only an environmental issue, but also a challenge with significant social and economic dimensions. Its prevention, together with the adoption of healthy and sustainable dietary patterns, represents two closely interconnected prerequisites for transitioning towards food systems that use resources more efficiently and ensure adequate, healthy and affordable nutrition for all.

In this direction, EATWISE draws on the research findings and expertise of previous and ongoing projects of the Harokopio University team, translating scientific knowledge into practical, accessible and applicable solutions. The project develops three interconnected education and communication programmes:

- the **EATWISE Training Kit**, for primary and secondary school teachers and their pupils;
- the **EATWISE Course Module**, for university students and a wide range of professionals and stakeholders; and
- the **EATWISE Communication Platform** — the website you are currently visiting — for the general public.
