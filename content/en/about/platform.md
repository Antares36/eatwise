## An open hub for knowledge and action

The EATWISE Platform brings together the resources needed to understand and prevent food waste, while connecting everyday food choices with health, the environment and society.

### [Learn & Take Action →]({{< relLangURL "learn/" >}})

Practical, evidence-based guidance on smart food shopping, appropriate food storage, using leftovers and adopting more sustainable dietary habits.

### [For Educators →]({{< relLangURL "educators/" >}})

Free educational resources, the EATWISE Training Kit and activities for pupils aged 10 to 15 years.

### [Knowledge Hub →]({{< relLangURL "knowledge/" >}})

Scientific publications, infographics, podcasts and resources from related research projects.

### [Press Corner →]({{< relLangURL "press/" >}})

Press releases, media coverage, photos and materials for journalists and collaborating organisations.
