## Change can be learned, cultivated and shared

The EATWISE Training Kit is being developed to support educators who wish to incorporate food waste prevention, sustainable food consumption and nutrition into the learning experience.

The Kit is primarily addressed to primary and secondary school teachers and includes activities for pupils aged 10 to 15 years. It will be freely available in digital format, with clear implementation guidance and flexibility to adapt activities to the needs of each classroom.

The material is being developed in collaboration with educators to ensure that it is useful, practical and appropriate for the school setting.
