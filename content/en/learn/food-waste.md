---

Food waste is not only an environmental issue. It also has economic, social and nutritional implications. When food that could have been eaten is discarded, the resources needed to produce, transport, store and prepare it are also wasted.

Prevention starts with three simple actions: plan, store food appropriately, and use what you already have in time.
